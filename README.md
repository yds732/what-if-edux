# ECHO: Between Two Worlds

EduX 2026 UK, Track 4: *Immersive Worlds for Future Citizenship*.

It's 2080, and you volunteer to test **Echo**, an AI companion headset, for one school week. In **Haven**, a dreamlike virtual world, your companion Lumi agrees with everything you do. In the real world, your classmate Jamie sometimes disagrees, and sometimes needs you. Each day sets your own needs against someone else's. On Friday you help your city decide who should get Echo.

The game is for ages 11 to 14 and takes a few minutes to play. It is based on Carol Gilligan's ethics of care. No choice is marked right or wrong, and there is no score. The aim is for players to notice whom each choice cares for, and whose needs it leaves out. The story moves from **self** to **other** to **community**.

**Play it:** https://echo-two-worlds.vercel.app

## Technical plan

- **The game lives in [`echo/`](echo/).** It is a complete, deployed build, written in TypeScript with three.js and Vite. Everything you see and hear is generated by code. It runs in a desktop browser, on phones and tablets, and in WebXR headsets. [`echo/README.md`](echo/README.md) explains how to run, test and deploy it.
- **The design document is the source of truth.** Every line of dialogue is in `echo/src/story/script.ts`, and each day is a short script in `echo/src/story/chapters/`. Changes to the story go there first.
- **The branching stays small on purpose.** There are three decisions (Tuesday, Thursday and the Friday vote). Only the Friday vote changes the ending, and the other paths join back up. This keeps the game short and the testing manageable.
- **Remaining work is mostly art and polish:** clearer characters (Lumi, Nova, Kai and Jamie), reading faces and expressions in conversation, and the pitch video. If we bring in generated avatars or the MANI Engine, they replace the procedural characters behind the existing character interface. The story and game code stay the same.
- **The root folder is the earlier "What If" prototype** (`index.html`, `src/`, `data/`, `docs/`). It is kept for reference only and is not part of ECHO.
